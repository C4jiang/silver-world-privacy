Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Silver World (the “App”), published by C14chan (the “Developer,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), including its Meta Quest version.

We do not collect, use, store, or share user data through the App.

1. Data collection and use

We do not collect, receive, retain, or use personal information or other user data through the App. This includes names, email addresses, Meta account identifiers, device identifiers, location data, usage analytics, and crash reports. We do not operate a service that receives user data from the App.

2. Data sharing and third-party services

We do not sell, share, or disclose user data. The App does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking services that collect user data on our behalf.

3. Device-local operation

Information processed on your device solely to run the App is not transmitted to or accessed by us. Any locally saved settings or progress remain on your device and are not collected by the Developer.

4. Data deletion

Because we do not collect or store user data, we hold no user data to access or delete. If the App saves settings or progress locally, you can remove that information by clearing the App’s data through your device settings, where available. For information held by the platform provider, please use that provider’s privacy controls.

5. Platform and website privacy

Meta may independently process information when you use its devices, accounts, or store, under the Meta Privacy Policy. This is separate from the Developer’s data practices described here.

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information when serving this website, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. This page contains no analytics, tracking scripts, forms, or third-party embedded content.

6. Children’s privacy

We do not collect personal information from any users, including children.

7. Changes to this policy

If our data practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date before those changes take effect.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Developer through the Silver World game page or submit a privacy question on GitHub. GitHub issues are public; please do not include personal or sensitive information.
